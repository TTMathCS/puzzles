## What the mathematics gives you

**A count.** How many moves does the Tower of Hanoi need? How many ways are there to place eight queens? Counting
turns "it takes a while" into an exact number, and often shows why the number grows so fast.
See [Counting](../theory/counting.md).

**A proof of impossibility.** Some puzzles can't be solved, and no amount of trying will show that. A single clever
quantity that never changes, an **invariant**, can. The 15 puzzle and the bridges of Königsberg are the two most
famous examples. See [Invariants and parity](../theory/invariants.md).

**A method.** Backtracking for the queens, a rule of thumb for the knight, a few lines of algebra for Lights Out:
each puzzle comes with a way of solving every case, not just the one in front of you.
See [Search and backtracking](../theory/search.md).
