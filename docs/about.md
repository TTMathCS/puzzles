# About this site

*What it is, who made it, and how to use it*

**Puzzles** is a small, free site about puzzles: the classic ones, played on the page, and the mathematics that
explains them. It belongs to [Mars – your new home](https://www.akadian.ca/), a calm, imagined city on Mars that its
owner is building to share with students and friends, and it sits beside the sister sites
[Algorithms](https://cs.akadian.ca/), [Math](https://math.akadian.ca/) and [Cryptography](https://cryptography.akadian.ca/).

## AI-created

Every page here was written by an AI, and the playable puzzles and the banner picture were programmed by an AI. That
is why each page carries a small **AI-created** tag. The facts were checked against the books and papers listed,
closed, at the end of each page; if you find a mistake, it is a mistake of ours, and the
[repository](https://github.com/TTMathCS/puzzles) is open for corrections.

## How a page is built

1. **Try it.** Each page starts with the puzzle itself: on the page when it can be played, in words when it can't.
2. **How to think about it.** The idea that cracks the puzzle, in plain words.
3. **Why it works.** The mathematics, kept short.
4. **Going deeper.** Closed boxes with proofs, variations and history, for those who want more.
5. **Sources.** Closed at the end of the page.

Answers are always closed, so you choose when to look. The pages are short, five or six minutes each; a long page
splits into parts you move through with the bar at the bottom. The **Aa** button in the header changes the text size
and the light.

## Nothing is collected

The puzzles run in your browser. Nothing you do on this site is saved on a server or sent anywhere, and there is no
sign-in.

## Levels

The badge under each title uses the same three levels as the sister sites. **Beginner**: no background needed, good
for young readers and classes. **Intermediate**: comfortable with school math. **Expert**: the page reaches university
mathematics or current research.
